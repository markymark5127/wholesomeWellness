Assisted Lymphatic Therapy (ALT)

Assisted Lymphatic Therapy uses a specialized device to deliver gentle, relaxing, non-invasive motions across the body, helping to restore healthy lymphatic flow and support the body’s natural detoxification and immune functions.

What Is The Lymphatic System?

The lymphatic system is a crucial part of your immune system, consisting of lymph fluid, lymph nodes, the spleen, thymus, tonsils, and bone marrow. It transports white blood cells and removes waste from every cell in the body. When it's congested, your body can’t detox efficiently.

How Does It Get Congested?

  • Lack of physical activity
  • Emotional stress or trauma
  • Poor diet
  • Hormone imbalance
  • Environmental toxins
  • Excessive weight
  • Tight undergarments
  • Aging
  • Lymph node damage or removal

Why Is Congestion A Problem?

Lymphatic congestion causes low oxygenation and stagnant lymph, which leads to cellular degeneration, chronic inflammation, and a higher risk of disease.
