Meet Dawn Stoner, MS, OTR/L, CHHP

Founder of WholeSoul Wellness

With over 20 years of experience in rehabilitation as a licensed Occupational Therapist, I’ve had the privilege of guiding thousands of clients through recovery in hospital, acute, and outpatient settings.

In addition to my clinical background, I am a Certified Holistic Health Practitioner. My personal experiences—navigating health challenges within my own family—ignited a deep passion for natural healing and wellness.

At WholeSoul Wellness, I bring a unique blend of medical insight and natural healing. I believe the human body is inherently capable of healing when the right conditions are created. My mission is to support whole-person wellness by listening deeply to your concerns, educating you along the way, and tailoring your healing journey to meet your personal goals.

Wellness is not one-size-fits-all—it’s personal. I look forward to working with you, one-on-one, to help you feel your best and live life with intention and vitality.
